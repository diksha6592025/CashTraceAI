Cyber safety

Safety Center

Practical guidance for preserving evidence and responding to suspicious digital transactions.

Preserve evidence

Keep transaction references, messages, timestamps and relevant screenshots. Avoid altering original evidence where possible.

Use official channels

Contact your bank and the appropriate cybercrime reporting channel promptly when financial loss or unauthorized activity is suspected.

Protect access

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or remote-access credentials with unknown callers or messages.

Verify before acting

Treat predictive or automated signals as assistance. Confirm important facts through authorized sources before intervention.
