CashTrace AI

About the system

A prototype for predictive cybercrime investigation support.
Purpose

From complaint to probable cash-out

CashTrace AI organizes authorized transaction evidence into a fund-lineage view, adds behavioral risk signals and produces a probable cash-out location and time window for investigator review.

Responsible AI

Human-led decisions

Outputs are probabilistic. They should not be treated as proof of wrongdoing, and operational actions remain with authorized human investigators.
